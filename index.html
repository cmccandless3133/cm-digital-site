Straight answers

The questions I actually get

Short versions, no runaround. Anything I've missed, ask me or ask the AI line.

When do text alerts start?
From day one, every lead, booking and call summary comes to your email right away. Texts from your business number (missed-call text-backs, text reminders and texted review links) need a carrier registration called 10DLC: usually $25 one time plus $15 a month, and 1 to 2 weeks to approve, sometimes longer. I file the paperwork for you on day one.
Can I add something that isn't in a package?
Yes. Extra service or location pages are $99 each, a past-customer reactivation blast is $250 one time, and custom automations are $75 an hour, quoted up front.
Do I actually own it?
Yes, all of it. The domain is registered in your name, the site runs on your own hosting account, and you get every login. The content and your customer list are yours. If you ever want to move it or hand it to somebody else, it goes with you and I don't have to be involved.
Is the hosting really free?
Yes, for a normal small-business site. It runs on a free hosting tier under your account, and a site like yours stays inside it. The only thing you pay for yearly is the domain name, which runs about $12 to $20 and goes straight to the registrar, not to me.
What if you disappear?
Fair question to ask anybody. Your site is plain, self-contained files sitting in your own account, so any developer can pick it up — there's nothing proprietary to get stuck in. You'd keep running without me. The automations and the AI line are the piece that needs someone watching them, and that's what the monthly covers; cancel it and the website keeps working.
Can I edit it myself?
Most owners don't want to, so my default is you text me the change and it's done. If you'd rather do it yourself, say so up front and I'll build it so the parts you'll actually touch — prices, hours, photos, specials — are editable without me.
What does a change cost later?
Small stuff like hours, a price, a photo or a new service is covered by your package: up to 30 minutes of updates a month on The Foundation and The Lead Engine, and up to an hour on The 24/7 Front Desk. Past that it's $75 an hour, one hour minimum, and I tell you the number before I start.
Are you going to upsell me?
No. All three packages and their prices are on this page, so there's no surprise number later. The Foundation is $499 setup + $49 a month, and if that's all you need, that's all I'll sell you. The bigger packages add tools that take ongoing work to run, like booking, quote follow-ups and the phone AI, and that's why their monthly is higher. I'll tell you if I think you don't need them.
Will I show up first on Google?
I'm not going to promise that, and be careful with anybody who does. What I can do is the part that's in my control: your site built so Google can read it, your Google Business Profile set up right, your towns and services on the page, and a review link so customers can leave you reviews. Ranking at the top of a competitive search takes months of steady work. For most shops around here the bigger win is being findable and easy to call, which happens fast.
How long does it take?
For most builds you'll have a working sample to look at in a couple of days. Something big or unusual takes longer, and I'll tell you up front if it does. After you've looked at it and want to go ahead, it's live within a few days — most of that wait is you getting me photos and your service list. The AI receptionist can be answering your line the next day.
Do we have to meet in person?
No. I build it, send you a link, and you look at it on your phone. All I need is a few photos and about ten minutes on what you do. I'm in Winslow, Indiana, so if you're close and would rather sit down in person, I'm glad to, but it's an offer, not a requirement.
